01

Your site is live. But nobody can find it yet.

Most small business websites sit invisible on page 4 of Google — not because they're bad, but because Google doesn't know they exist. Without the right foundation, you're paying for a site that never earns its keep. We fix that from day one: Search Console connected, sitemap submitted, keywords dialed in, and your site structured the way Google rewards.

02

Google Maps is where local customers decide.

When someone searches "barber near me" or "best trainer in [city]" — the first thing they see is Google Maps. Your Google Business Profile is your storefront on the internet's busiest street corner. We set it up right: verified, keyword-optimized, loaded with photos, and posting consistently so Google keeps pushing you to the top of the local pack.

03

AI search is the new front door. You need to be in it.

People don't just Google anymore. They ask ChatGPT. They ask Claude. They ask Perplexity. These AI tools scan the web and recommend businesses by name. If your site isn't structured the right way — with the right content, schema markup, and authority signals — you're invisible to an entirely new generation of search. We optimize everything so AI tools recommend your business when someone asks for exactly what you do.

04

Content is what keeps you ranked. AI makes it effortless.

Google rewards fresh, relevant content. Without it, even a perfectly optimized site slowly slides down the rankings. We use AI to generate SEO-optimized content for your business every single month — blog posts, service page updates, Google Business posts — all written to rank, all published for you automatically. You focus on running your business. We keep feeding the machine that feeds you customers.
